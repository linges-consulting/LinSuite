# M4 — Money: billing, packages, commission and retail

## Problem Statement

LinSuite can schedule and complete appointments, maintain customer records, capture forms and session notes, and send notifications. Staff still need a reliable way to turn completed services and retail sales into reviewed bills, record payments, issue accurate documents, and manage prepaid credits and stock. Owners need control over exceptions and visibility into earned commission, unpaid balances and undelivered prepaid services.

An appointment price alone does not cover checkout. A price may need approval, discounts may affect commission differently, an insurer may approve a payment that has not arrived, and a package redemption may involve no new payment. Editing issued documents or treating those situations as ordinary paid sales would lose financial history, misstate balances and risk deducting credits or stock twice.

The business needs a simple staff workflow with administrative approval where required, accurate manual payment recording, and durable history. Insurance submission and payment processing continue through the business's existing external systems.

## Solution

Completing an appointment automatically creates or contributes to a draft service bill. Staff review the bill, apply eligible predefined discounts, and submit exceptional changes for admin/owner review. Alternatively, an admin/owner can authenticate on the staff screen, edit that bill, save, and return control to staff. The two override paths are independently configurable and enabled by default.

After review, staff issue the invoice and complete checkout. They record payments manually and collect the client portion before the client leaves, unless an admin/owner authorizes an outstanding client balance with a reason. External insurer approval is recorded as a pending payer allocation; it becomes a payment only when funds actually arrive. Final treatment receipts retain the date of service and show the approved charge, prepaid value and actual payment status accurately.

Packages and bundles supply prepaid service credits, activated only after full payment and deducted on appointment completion. Entirely unused purchases can be refunded; using the first credit makes the whole purchase nonrefundable except for an explicit admin/owner manual refund. Retail uses separate invoices, whole-unit stock, variant thresholds and atomic stock deductions when an invoice is issued.

Issued invoices are corrected through cancellation and replacement. Payments, credits, stock movements and commission postings retain their history. Admin/owner reports show earned commission separately from cash received and pending payments, along with unused package value. Staff can print or download documents and email PDF attachments through M3's verified sender.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Architecture and scope.** Extend the existing modular monolith: billing owns bills, invoices, payments, discounts, package purchases/redemptions and commission reporting; inventory owns products, variants and stock movements. Integrate scheduling completion, customer history, auth capabilities, the shared document boundary and M3 notifications. Keep one business per deployment and support on-prem installations.
- **Vocabulary.** A draft bill is editable and unissued. An invoice is the issued financial document. A payment receipt is a render of the invoice's recorded payments, not a third document lifecycle. A treatment receipt represents one delivered appointment. A package contains credits for one service; a bundle contains credits for multiple explicitly eligible services. A pending payer allocation is not a payment and is not an insurance claim.
- **Completion transaction.** The appointment's transition to completed, service commission-rate snapshot, selected-credit redemption, draft line creation and audit event must commit consistently. Use the existing terminal completion boundary and appointment identity to prevent duplicate effects. Cancellation and no-show do not consume credits or create completed-service commission.
- **Visit grouping.** Reuse the existing booking group where present. Completed appointments for the same visit contribute distinct provider/service lines to a compatible open service draft. Never append to an issued invoice; a later completion requires an unissued draft. Treatment receipts remain per appointment.
- **Draft pricing.** Start with the existing appointment service-price snapshot. Staff confirm the price during post-completion review. A selected prepaid redemption contributes its fixed attributable value and prepaid settlement, not a new amount to collect. Current catalog changes must not rewrite existing appointment or purchased-credit values.
- **Review authority.** Enabled, eligible predefined discounts can be applied by staff. Ad hoc discounts and price overrides require admin/owner approval. Administrative review may approve or revise the proposal; staff then resume billing. Persist the requested change, requester, reviewer, decision, affected draft revision and timestamps. Changes requiring approval cannot be issued under a stale approval.
- **Inline authority.** The admin/owner supplies their own credentials on the staff screen. Reuse existing authentication, throttling, revocation and configured MFA policy; do not silently waive MFA or change the staff session into an unrestricted admin session. Privileged editing is limited to the reviewed bill and ends after save/cancel or the administrative authentication window expires. Attribute privileged changes to the admin/owner and subsequent ordinary billing to staff.
- **Override configuration.** Two independent business switches control staff review requests and inline administrative editing. Both default to enabled. Enforce them on the server as well as the screen. Disabling a path prevents new use of that path and does not remove an admin/owner's own billing access or erase existing request history.
- **Capabilities.** Follow the existing capability registry and fresh-per-request checks, rather than hard-coded role-name branches. Administrative bill overrides, refund approval, discount/configuration management and commission visibility require the appropriate administrative capability and valid administrative authority. Staff cancellation and payment-entry correction are ordinary billing permissions. Stock receiving and manual count correction are distinct permissions, defaulting to admin/owner and independently grantable to trusted staff.
- **Discount definitions.** Support percentage and fixed-amount discounts, enabled state, eligibility across service/product/package categories and all-or-selected items, stackability and commission treatment. Persist the selected rule and resolved amounts on the bill; later definition edits must not change issued history.
- **Discount ordering.** All discounts in a combination must permit stacking. Apply percentage discounts sequentially, followed by fixed amounts, independently of selection order. Use deterministic ordering and cent rounding. For example, 100 reduced by 10% then 20% becomes 72, not 70. Reject combinations exceeding the eligible charge; do not silently clamp to zero. An explicit zero-price override still requires administrative approval.
- **Discount commission treatment.** Each discount independently either reduces the commission basis or is absorbed by the business. On a 100 charge, a business-funded 10 discount followed by a commission-reducing 20 discount yields a client charge of 70 and commission basis of 80. A one-off price override exposes the same business decision and defaults to commission on the revised price. Tax is excluded from commission.
- **Money and tax.** Store money as integer cents and percentage rates in the existing integer-rate convention. Tax components are effective-dated data selected from the business jurisdiction and enabled independently per item. Support tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive catalog/override entry, defaulting to before-tax pricing, with clear labels. Compute tax per line, round half-up to cents and sum; snapshot resolved components, rates, amounts and price convention at invoice issue. Ensure inclusive-price extraction and component totals reconcile exactly. Do not infer service tax exemption from a practitioner's profession.
- **Invoice issue.** Issue only a valid reviewed draft with required approvals, current authorization and consistent totals. Allocate the business invoice number atomically at issue; cancellation preserves the original number and document. Preserve original line identities, amounts, discounts, tax, attribution and approved commission choices. Read/render operations must not recompute issued amounts from live definitions.
- **Payment ledger.** Record received payments with amount, client/insurer payer, method, reference, collector and timestamp. Support split payments and derive outstanding balance from the ledger and prepaid allocations. Payment facts, corrections, refunds and allocation transfers are append-only and traceable; an invoice's paid state is not an independently editable boolean.
- **Insurance simplification.** Claims, approvals and follow-up happen in external systems. LinSuite records only the relevant payer/reference, approved pending portion and actual receipts against ordinary invoices. It does not implement submitted/adjudicated/denied claim states or an expected-versus-adjudicated claim engine. An external approval permits the approved insurer portion to remain pending at checkout but does not mark it received. Record a payment only when money arrives.
- **Checkout gate.** The client portion must be fully settled before normal checkout completion. An admin/owner can authorize an outstanding client balance with a recorded reason. Pending approved insurer money does not by itself block checkout once the client portion is settled, but remains visibly unpaid. Ordinary billing lists and client history support manual balance follow-up without an insurance-specific workflow.
- **Payment correction versus refund.** Staff may correct an erroneous payment entry, including amount, with a required reason. Preserve the original and append the correction/replacement linkage, then recompute the balance. This records a clerical correction; it does not authorize moving money back to the client. Actual refunds require admin/owner approval. Correction and refund concurrency must not violate the refund cap or leave inconsistent allocations.
- **Cancel and replace.** Staff may cancel an issued invoice with a required reason. Atomically retain and mark the original cancelled, create a replacement draft using its details, link the lineage and carry payment allocations with transfer history. Collect any increased balance through ordinary payments; route a required refund through administrative approval. Replacing an invoice must not charge the client again for a carried payment.
- **Replacement side effects.** Invoice cancellation does not undo the completed appointment or make delivered goods appear physically returned. Preserve the underlying redemption/fulfilment identity across replacements. Do not deduct the same package credit or stock unit twice, allocate the same payment twice, or count original and replacement commission together. Use reversing/replacement financial postings and explicit additional-sale or stock-return movements for real changes.
- **Package purchase model.** Support package/bundle definitions and purchased snapshots containing the paid price, required payment, eligible services, credit quantities, per-session values, optional expiry and current credit entitlement. Credits remain pending until the purchase is fully paid. Booking and no-show leave entitlement unchanged; completion performs guarded redemption and writes its append-only movement in the same transaction.
- **Bundle value.** Allocate the purchased service value proportionally to regular service prices captured at purchase. An assessment priced at 120 and two follow-ups at 60 have weights 120:60:60; a 200 bundle allocates 100:50:50. Use deterministic cent remainders so values sum to the purchased amount. Freeze these values for redemption, treatment receipts, commission and unused-value reporting. Redemption does not silently apply a second purchase discount or rewrite the purchase snapshot.
- **Standard package refunds.** If no credit has ever been redeemed, the package/bundle is refundable for the amount actually paid, less any earlier refund. A normal full unused-purchase refund cancels its unused entitlement. Once any credit is used, the entire package/bundle is nonrefundable under the standard policy. Do not retain the older paid-minus-used-at-regular-price partial-refund formula.
- **Manual refund exceptions.** An admin/owner may specify a manual amount for goodwill or dispute resolution, including after package use. Require a reason. Total refunds cannot exceed actual payments received minus prior refunds, including through invoice replacement lineages. For packages/bundles, explicitly choose to keep or cancel unused credits; cancellation defaults on a full refund. Explicitly choose commission preservation or reversal; preserving earned commission is the default for a goodwill refund. Log the amount, reason, approver and both choices.
- **Expiry and transfer.** Package expiry is optional and disabled by default. Packages/bundles are non-transferable by default; an administrative exception requires an audit entry. No mixed service/product prepaid packages or retail credit bundles are introduced.
- **Commission snapshots and timing.** Retain separate service and retail rates per staff member. Capture the service rate at appointment completion for both ordinary and package services, not at invoice issue. When the reviewed bill is finalized, calculate/post earned commission using that frozen rate and final approved pre-tax basis. Package commission belongs to the delivering practitioner and is earned on delivery, not package sale. Record the retail seller's rate and basis at retail invoice issue.
- **Retail attribution.** A retail invoice has a "Sold by" staff selection defaulting to the staff member handling the sale; staff can select the actual seller. Store the payment collector separately. Changing the collector does not change the seller's commission attribution.
- **Commission reporting.** Restrict rates, amounts and report responses to administrative access; staff-facing bill, payment, roster and document payloads must not leak them. Report earned service/retail revenue and commission by date range and staff with CSV export. Show received and pending payments separately from earned commission. Refunds and replacements post adjustments in the period they occur; manual refund commission choices govern their effect. Do not count payment allocation transfers as new cash receipts.
- **Package liability report.** Show unused eligible credits and their fixed purchase values, accounting for redemption, cancellation, expiry and explicit manual-refund credit decisions. A goodwill refund that preserves credits must not silently erase the remaining entitlement from the report.
- **Inventory schema and invariants.** Products have variants, SKUs, barcodes, prices, tax settings, whole-unit stock and per-variant thresholds. Stock movements retain the sale/return/receipt/adjustment identity, signed whole-unit change, actor, reason and timestamp. Quantities cannot become negative.
- **Stock issue boundary.** Retail draft edits neither deduct nor reserve stock. At invoice issue, revalidate availability and atomically apply guarded stock deductions with their movement records and invoice issue. Concurrent buyers cannot both acquire the last unit. Failure leaves the invoice, stock, movements and payments in a consistent state.
- **Receiving and adjustment.** Stock receiving and count corrections are separate server-enforced permissions. Ordinary sale deductions do not grant either permission. Preserve manual movement reasons and attribution; do not replace movement history with an editable stock log.
- **Retail customers and returns.** Client linking is optional for retail; linked purchases appear in client history, while anonymous sales do not require a fabricated customer. Refund money and restock goods independently. The return-to-stock choice remains off for unsuitable opened/damaged goods; allow an explicit suitable return when selected. Apply whole-unit return movements and administrative refund approval.
- **Low stock.** Each variant owns its threshold and alert state. Produce in-app warnings and optional email to opted-in admin/owner recipients when it crosses below the threshold. Do not send on every subsequent sale below that threshold. Restocking above the threshold rearms the next crossing. Evaluate the crossing transactionally, enqueue delivery after commit, and deduplicate retries so concurrency does not create repeated warnings.
- **Treatment receipt timing.** Completion creates the billing facts and draft, not the final client-facing receipt. Release the final treatment receipt after bill approval and checkout completion, retaining the original service date, appointment/provider identity, final attributable value and correct payment status. Pending insurer amounts remain pending. A prepaid session shows its allocated value without representing a new payment on that service date. Multi-provider visits retain separate appointment receipts.
- **Receipt contents.** Include business identity/contact and configured registration/footer details, document identity, client identity where applicable, service date, service/provider, final charge or prepaid attributable value, tax breakdown and payment status. Clinical treatment receipts require the practitioner's designation/licence snapshot; nonclinical businesses are not forced to invent clinical credentials. Payment receipts remain views of the original invoice plus immutable ledger history.
- **Financial document storage.** Reuse the shared document encryption/integrity boundary and encrypted PostgreSQL byte storage; no filesystem or cloud-only document store. Its current customer-key ownership must be extended for billing-owned financial records and anonymous retail. Financial documents must satisfy their own retention requirements and must not become unreadable merely because a clinical/customer key is shredded. Do not weaken existing customer-document foreign keys, customer suppression guards, purge-role restrictions or clinical retention transactions. Any required ADR amendment must be explicit rather than silently rewriting an accepted clinical rule.
- **Background work.** Render PDFs, deliver notifications and generate exports through the existing Celery infrastructure, outside request handlers. Queue after durable state commits and reconcile missed work. Retries must not create extra invoice numbers, financial effects, archives or stock alerts. A rendering or email failure must not undo a committed checkout.
- **PDF email attachments.** Extend the existing email provider contract, real Resend/SMTP adapters, worker dispatch and recording provider to support attachment metadata and PDF bytes. Preserve ordinary email callers. Use the configured verified business sender; do not silently count console fallback as delivered. Staff explicitly trigger invoice/receipt delivery to an appropriate recipient, including an entered recipient for an anonymous retail document when needed. Print/download remain available without a verified sender. Queue identifiers for authorized archived documents and fetch bytes inside the worker, rather than putting sensitive document bytes in queue messages.
- **Audit and database enforcement.** Record actor, requester/approver distinction, resource identity, reason where required, and timestamp for financial exceptions and ledger/stock/credit changes. Apply explicit transactional access auditing when a client-linked financial document is opened, following the existing read-audit pattern. Protect issued amounts and append-only history using database grants/triggers and guarded updates, not only application checks. Preserve the distinct application, migration and purge authorities.
- **API and UI contracts.** Expose capability-checked actions for draft review, request/decision, scoped inline editing, issue, checkout, manual payment/correction, cancellation/replacement, approved refund, package selection/redemption, retail issue, stock receipt/adjustment, documents and administrative reports. Return actionable validation/conflict errors and refreshed balances. Do not show administrative commission details or transport internals in ordinary staff checkout.

## Testing Decisions

- **Proposed primary seam: S1.** Test the feature through authenticated HTTP requests against the existing migrated real PostgreSQL harness, connected as the production application role. Prefer this one high-level boundary for complete billing, retail, package, permissions and reporting behaviors. Do not mock the database or introduce a new internal billing test interface.
- **Existing supporting seams.** Use S2 only for focused deterministic money/tax/discount/allocation calculations; S3's recording notification provider for actual dispatched emails and attachments; the existing rendered document boundary for receipt content; and S5 real-role database/concurrency checks where HTTP alone cannot prove grants or race safety. These are established repository seams, not new feature-specific harnesses.
- **Frontend boundary.** Use the existing React Testing Library/Vitest user-interaction approach with controlled HTTP responses. Exercise visible actions, labels, totals, validation and mode transitions rather than component internals or exact request-call counts.
- **Good tests.** Assert user-visible results and durable external effects: totals, balances, document content, permissions, recorded deliveries, stock/credit counts and audit history. Do not mirror helper implementations, patch transaction internals or test private method choreography. Use independent expected amounts for calculation examples.
- **Prior art.** Follow appointment completion/group/concurrency HTTP tests, administrative-mode authentication tests, staff commission-field visibility tests, encrypted document and archived-form HTTP tests, document grant/trigger tests, notification trigger/dispatch/recording-provider tests, and scheduling lifecycle/frontend mode-refetch tests. Reuse their fixtures and production-role posture.
- **Completion and checkout.** Cover automatic draft creation, grouped provider lines, package versus ordinary payment, completion retry, cancellation/no-show, post-completion price confirmation, approval-required issue rejection, client-payment gate and an authorized outstanding-balance exception. Receipt release must not happen before review and checkout.
- **Review and inline authentication.** Cover staff requests, administrative revision/approval, incorrect credentials, configured MFA, expired/revoked authority, bill-specific authority, returning to the original staff session, both independent configuration switches and server-side bypass attempts. A stale draft approval cannot authorize a different exceptional change.
- **Discount arithmetic.** Cover 100 with sequential 10% and 20% yielding 72, percentages before fixed amounts, nonstackable conflicts, eligibility, excessive combinations, mixed commission choices yielding client charge 70 and commission basis 80, revised-price default, explicit zero-price approval, cent remainders and unchanged historical definitions.
- **Tax arithmetic.** Cover inclusive/exclusive entry, multiple independent components, exempt components, effective-date boundaries, half-cent rounding, line-versus-total reconciliation and issued snapshots after settings change. Test configured input rates rather than assuming a remembered tax rate is current law.
- **Insurance and manual payments.** Cover split payer/method entries, references, externally approved but unpaid insurer amounts, actual subsequent insurer receipt, accurate paid/pending documents and ordinary outstanding balances. Assert that no approval produces a fabricated received payment or claim-state workflow.
- **Payment corrections and refunds.** Cover staff correcting an amount/method/reference with a reason and immutable originals, staff refund denial, approved refund, prior-refund cap, replacement-lineage cap, concurrency between refunds/corrections and ledger-derived balances.
- **Cancel and replace.** Cover staff cancellation reasons, automatic replacement drafts, original preservation, payment transfers, collecting an increased difference, administrative refund for a decrease, no duplicated stock/credits/earned commission, and retries that preserve one lineage/effect.
- **Packages and bundles.** Cover payment activation, eligible selection before completion, no booking/no-show deduction, last-credit races, frozen proportional allocation summing to the purchase amount, prepaid receipt value, unused full refund, first-credit nonrefundability, manual exceptions, prior-refund cap, explicit remaining-credit and commission choices, optional expiry and audited transfer.
- **Commission and reporting.** Cover a completion rate of 40% remaining 40% after a change to 50% before invoice issue; correct final discounted/business-funded basis; package delivery rather than purchase attribution; retail seller distinct from collector; earned versus received/pending figures; administrative-only endpoints and staff payload exclusions; refund/replacement posting dates and CSV reconciliation.
- **Inventory and returns.** Cover whole-unit validation, no draft reservation, atomic final issue, two buyers competing for the last unit, movement/invoice rollback on failure, receiving versus count-adjustment permissions, client-linked/anonymous retail and a refund without restocking an opened product.
- **Threshold alerts.** Cover independent variant thresholds, one event/email per crossing, no repeated warning while already below threshold, restocking above the threshold, a later crossing, concurrent reductions, disabled email preference, unverified sender and retried delivery.
- **Documents and email.** Inspect meaningful rendered content and print layout, including provider/date, approved totals, prepaid values, pending insurer amounts and separated invoice/treatment document identities. Verify attachment filename/type/content through the recording provider and real adapter contract tests, while never sending production email from the suite. Cover queued rendering, failed enqueue reconciliation, delivery failures/retry, archive integrity and no duplicate financial action on resend.
- **Retention and access.** Verify billing-owned financial documents remain retrievable under their retention contract, anonymous documents do not require a fake customer, chart-key deletion cannot silently destroy retained invoices, financial access is audited, cross-customer/document access fails, and clinical purge/foreign-key protections still hold.
- **Database enforcement.** Use real application and purge roles to attempt forbidden edits/deletes of issued values and append-only records. Check guarded nonnegative stock/credits, invoice numbering races, allocation/refund caps and completion idempotency under concurrency. Migrations must preserve the existing document and audit protections.
- **Validation scope.** During implementation, run focused tests for each vertical behavior and the required backend/frontend lint, type, build, migration and regression checks. This specification task changes no application behavior and does not claim that M4 implementation tests have run.

## Out of Scope

- Insurance submission, insurer policy validation, coverage calculation, insurer adjudication states, insurer integrations and a dedicated claim/underpayment dashboard.
- Payment gateways, automatic card charging, payment-provider settlement, payroll, payouts, withholding and pay-period processing.
- Tips, gratuity, memberships, recurring billing and booth/chair rent.
- Combined service/retail invoices, mixed service/product prepaid packages and retail credit bundles. Retail promotions use discounts; a stocked gift set remains a product.
- Standard partial-use package refunds calculated from regular-price sessions. Only the explicit administrative manual exception permits a refund after first use.
- Fractional stock units, draft stock reservation, cross-business data access, multi-tenant shared billing and cloud-only document storage.
- Shipping/location-dependent tax calculation, expanded purchasing/procurement workflows and new barcode hardware integrations.
- Automatic document emails without a staff action, invoice/receipt SMS delivery and automatic insurer claim submission from emailed receipts.
- M4 code implementation, deployment or a new implementation ticket breakdown as part of publishing this specification.
